feat(extension): show the onboarding again from Settings

Settings › About has a new row, "Introduction", with a "Show again" button. It puts the popup's
onboarding back to its first step and opens the popup. Where the browser cannot open the popup
from Settings, the row says "Open Thaumic Cast from the toolbar to start." Finishing or skipping
it works as the first time. Settings, the address and saved sets are left as they are.
