feat(extension): light the toolbar icon while casting, in Chrome's light or dark colours

The extension's toolbar icon now matches Chrome: a deep purple on a light toolbar and a light
purple on a dark one, so it stands out as clearly as Chrome's own icons. While a cast from this
browser is running, the centre of the icon fills in, so you can see at a glance that sound is
going to your speakers without opening the popup. Until Chrome says whether it is light or dark,
the icon keeps the purple that reads on both.
